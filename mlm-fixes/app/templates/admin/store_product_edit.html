How plan matching works
  • Members add products to a cart. When the cart total equals a plan amount (₹1,800 / ₹3,600 / ₹7,200 / ₹14,400 / ₹28,800) that plan activates or upgrades and all plan benefits are paid.
  • Price products so common combinations land on these amounts (e.g. ₹450, ₹600, ₹900, ₹1,200, ₹1,800).
  • Later orders of any amount are repurchases (repurchase cashback + repurchase referral).
  • Cost price is never shown to members; it is only used for profit reporting.
